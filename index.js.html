Our Standards

OUR
COMMITMENT
TO QUALITY

At ASB, quality isn't a department — it's our culture. Every unit we produce goes through rigorous multi-stage inspection processes to ensure it leaves our facility meeting or exceeding international benchmarks.

Our triple ISO certification reflects our holistic approach to manufacturing excellence: superior products, environmental responsibility, and a safe workplace for every single team member.

  • Rigorous multi-point quality inspection at every stage
  • Certified welding processes — SMAW, GMAW, FCAW
  • Full material traceability from source to delivery
  • Customer acceptance testing conducted at our facility
  • Comprehensive quality documentation and audit trails
  • Trained and certified engineering team on-site
ISO9001

Quality Management System

ISO 9001:2015 certified. Every process from design to final delivery is governed by our QMS — ensuring consistent, measurable quality on every unit.

ISO14001

Environmental Management

ISO 14001:2015 certified. We minimize environmental impact across all manufacturing operations while maximizing sustainable efficiency.

ISO45001

Occupational Health & Safety

ISO 45001:2018 certified. Zero compromise on the wellbeing of our people — the highest occupational health and safety standards applied daily.

Our Journey

MILE
STONE

Over two decades of building, growing, and leading Indonesia's heavy transport and industrial equipment industry.

1998
Foundation
Company Founded

ASB was established in Karawang, West Java by a team of 12 craftsmen with a singular ambition — to build Indonesia's finest body trucks and heavy-duty equipment from the ground up.

Beginning of the journey
2003
Growth
First Major Government Contract

Secured a landmark contract with Indonesia's national logistics agency to deliver 50 custom box truck units — validating ASB's manufacturing capability and opening the door to large-scale industrial partnerships.

50 units delivered
2008
Quality
ISO 9001 Certification Achieved

Earned ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification — formalizing ASB's manufacturing processes and cementing our standing as an internationally recognized Indonesian manufacturer.

International quality recognition
2013
Expansion
SKID Equipment Division Launched

Expanded into Indonesia's booming oil & gas sector with a dedicated SKID-mounted equipment manufacturing division — diversifying ASB into energy, petrochemical, and industrial plant applications.

New industry, new frontier
2018
Landmark Year
New 15,000m² Campus & Triple ISO

Relocated to a purpose-built 15,000m² production campus and simultaneously achieved all three ISO certifications — ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 14001 (Environment), and ISO 45001 (Health & Safety) — a complete commitment to world-class manufacturing.

ISO 9001 · ISO 14001 · ISO 45001

Today
500+ Units Delivered & Still Growing
